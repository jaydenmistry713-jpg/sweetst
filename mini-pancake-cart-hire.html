Live cooking

A live pancake station, not a buffet table

Our mini pancake cart is cooked entirely on site. Batter goes on the griddle in front of your guests, and every portion is finished to order with the toppings and sauces they choose themselves — so nothing sits under a cloche going cold.

Each serving is five fluffy Dutch mini pancakes in a tray, ready to eat standing up with a drink in the other hand. That makes it easy to run alongside a reception, an evening dance floor or a corporate drinks hour without pulling people away from what they came for.

It's the cart people gather around. The cooking is the entertainment as much as the food, which is exactly why it works so well in the gap between a wedding breakfast and the evening.

Good to know

Frequently asked

Are your mini pancakes really eggless?

Yes — 100% eggless, every single batch, so they suit vegetarian guests as standard. Everything we serve is halal, and nut-free options are available. Just tell us about any allergies or dietary requirements when you book and we'll work around them.

How many pancakes does each guest get?

Each portion is five mini pancakes served in a tray, finished with your choice of toppings and sauces. Guests are welcome to come back for more during the service — most do.

Is there a minimum or maximum number of guests?

No. We cater intimate gatherings and large weddings alike, and we'll scale the service to suit. Tell us your numbers and we'll build the quote around them.

How long do you serve for?

A typical service window is two hours, and we arrive about an hour beforehand to set up. If your running order needs something different, tell us and we'll work to your timings.

What do you need from the venue?

One standard UK socket for the griddle, and enough floor space for the cart. We're happy to speak to your venue directly if they need details in advance.

How do we secure our date?

A 50% deposit confirms the booking and locks your date in. Weekend and wedding-season dates go early, so it's worth getting in touch as soon as you have a date in mind.
