Waffles made fresh, not held warm

Our waffle station cooks throughout your event rather than plating up in advance. Crisp on the outside, soft in the middle, and finished to order with whatever each guest points at — so every plate goes out hot.

It's the newest addition to the Sweet St. line-up and it has quickly become one of the most requested. The waffle cart carries exactly the same topping and sauce range as our mini pancake cart, which is why so many clients book the two together and let guests take one of each.

Everything is eggless as standard and halal, so a mixed guest list is covered without a separate menu or a special order.

Good to know

Frequently asked

Are your waffles eggless?

Yes. Like everything on our carts, the waffles are 100% eggless, which means they suit vegetarian guests as standard. Everything we serve is halal, and nut-free options are available — just let us know when you book.

What's the difference between the waffle station and the pancake cart?

The toppings and sauces are identical; the texture isn't. Waffles are crisp-edged and substantial, mini pancakes are light and fluffy. Plenty of clients book both carts together so guests can choose, and the two look striking side by side.

How much space does a waffle station need?

Enough floor space for the cart, plus a little room for guests to gather in front of it. We can speak to your venue directly if they need specifics before the day.

How long do you serve for?

A typical service window is two hours, with around an hour beforehand for setup. If your schedule needs a different slot, tell us and we'll build the timings around your running order.

Is there a minimum number of guests?

No minimum and no maximum. We scale the service to your numbers, so the waffle station works just as well for a small party as it does for a full wedding.

Do you cater corporate events?

Yes — office parties, product launches, client days and staff socials. The waffle station travels well and sets up quickly, which suits venues with tight access windows.
